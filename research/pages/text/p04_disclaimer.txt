10. Lagos Life Naira 
 The use of the term "Naira" and the symbol "₦" inside Lagos Life is part of a fictional game economy. 
 Any Naira amount displayed as a player's: 
cash; 
bank balance; 
salary; 
investment; 
transfer; 
property value; 
purchase; 
tax; 
reward; 
fine; 
gift; 
income; 
expense; or 
other game amount 
 is Game Naira , unless the interface clearly states that a real-money payment is being made. 
 Game Naira is not Nigerian legal tender. 
 It is not issued or guaranteed by the Central Bank of Nigeria. 
 It is not eNaira. 
 It is not a bank deposit. 
 It is not redeemable for Nigerian Naira. 
 It cannot be withdrawn from Lagos Life into a bank account. 
 It does not constitute stored real-world monetary value. 
 Game Naira may move between players within the Lagos Life game economy, but such transfers do not turn it into real money. 
 The use of familiar Nigerian currency terminology and the ₦ symbol is intended to make the fictional Nigerian game world understandable and immersive, not to represent Lagos Life as an issuer of Nigerian currency.
