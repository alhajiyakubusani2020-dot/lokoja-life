Start here: the game link and your first three steps 
 Lagos Life is a browser game, not an app. There is no App Store or Google Play download, so when you search for the Lagos Life app or the Lagos Life game app , this is it: the game running in Safari or Chrome at lagoslife.app , and also at lagoslife.eliysites.com . It is free, for players 18+, and you log in with the username and password you create while signing up. 
 Sign up (free, 18+). Open lagoslife.app, enter your Sim's name, then choose a username (3–20 letters, numbers or underscores) and a password of at least 8 characters. An email is optional; if you add one, you can log in with it as well as your username. 
 Create your Sim. Look → personality (two traits) → lifetime dream → birth lottery → your first home. 
 Take the birth lottery and start playing. The game decides once per account whether you are a Nepo Baby or a LAPO Baby; rent is then taken every Saturday. Pick a career, work your shifts and keep your six needs up.

What is Lagos Life? 
Lagos Life is a multiplayer life simulation game that runs in your web browser. You create a Sim, choose a home, work shifts, pay rent, make friends and date in one shared, Lagos-inspired city with other real players. Its creator, developer Shalom Rayhamen, has said the idea came from growing up playing The Sims 3 and from seeing Eureka's browser game Lagos Run, where you drive a danfo through Lagos ( post on X, 1 October 2026 ; Isakaba report ). The game's Terms of Use name him as its operator. 
 How popular is it? The game took off in early October 2026. The game's public stats page showed more than 1.1 million players and 8.4 million all-time visits when we checked on 5 October 2026, with over 62,000 people online at once. The creator reported more than 11,000 players online on 3 October, and a later Isakaba update reported more than 109,000 at the 1-million-player milestone. 
Lagos Life at a glance 
Where do I play? 
Open lagoslife.app in Safari or Chrome, then sign up for free. The same game also loads at its first address, lagoslife.eliysites.com. 
Is there an app? 
No App Store or Google Play app exists, and there's nothing to download. You can add Lagos Life to your home screen so it opens full screen with its own icon. 
What do you do? 
Build a Sim, work shifts, pay rent every Saturday, eat, sleep, hang out at real Lagos spots like Balogun Market, Elegushi Beach and Quilox, study at UNILAG, date, marry, start a business and vote in the weekly governor election. See the Lagos Life map and locations guide for all 31 of them, with opening hours and what you can do at each. 
Who is it for? 
Players aged 18+. You play in one shared Lagos with everyone else who signs up, so you can visit other players' houses, chat and send money. 
Getting started: the 5-step overview 
 Sign up at lagoslife.app. Pick a username and a password of 8+ characters. An email is optional and lets you log in with it too. 
 Create your Sim. Choose a look, two personality traits and a lifetime dream, such as Oga at the Top or Lekki Landlord. 
 Take the birth lottery. You don't choose this. The game makes you either a Nepo Baby or a LAPO Baby , once per account. 
 Pick a home. Mushin (₦2,400/week rent) or Yaba (₦6,000/week), or start as a UNILAG fresher in a hall of residence (₦1,200/week). Nepo Babies get a Lekki flat. Rent is taken every Saturday. 
 Get a job on your phone. Tap "Apply — start tomorrow", then show up for every shift. Our jobs table helps you pick.

Lagos Life FAQ: the basics 
 What is the Lagos Life game? 
Lagos Life is a free multiplayer life simulation game set in Lagos, Nigeria. You create a Sim, get a job, pay rent every Saturday, make friends and live in the same city as other real players. It runs in your web browser on phone or computer. What is the Lagos Life game link? 
Lagos Life currently loads at lagoslife.app , the address the game's own pages and in-game messages use. The same game is also reachable at lagoslife.eliysites.com , the address the developer has shared on X. When we checked on 5 October 2026, both showed the same live player counts. Be careful with look-alike sites, and never enter your password anywhere else. Is there a Lagos Life app on the App Store or Google Play? 
No. There is no App Store or Google Play app, and you don't need to download anything. Lagos Life runs in Safari or Chrome. To get an app-style icon, add it to your home screen. Our home screen guide shows how on iPhone and Android. Who made Lagos Life? Who is the owner? 
Lagos Life was made by developer Shalom Rayhamen (@Shalom_HeyEliy on X), and the game's own Terms of Use name him as its operator. Lagos Life Guide is a separate fan site with no link to them. Is Lagos Life free? 
Yes, signing up and playing are free. The game also has an optional wallet top-up that buys in-game naira with real money. You don't need it to follow any guide on this site. How old do you have to be to play? 
18 or older. You confirm your age when you sign up, and the Terms say accounts belonging to younger players can be closed. Are there Lagos Life codes? 
No. Lagos Life has no redeem code system right now, so any site offering "Lagos Life codes" is making them up. We'll add a page if the developer ever adds real codes. Can I withdraw or cash out my Lagos Life naira? 
No. The game's Terms of Use call its in-game naira a fictional game unit: it can't be withdrawn to a bank account or redeemed for real money, and players must not sell it for real money. Is Lagos Life on Roblox or Steam? 
No. Lagos Life is its own browser game. It isn't a Roblox experience or a Steam game. 
Last checked: 8 October 2026. Lagos Life is in active development, so numbers can change — if something looks different in your game, the game is right.
