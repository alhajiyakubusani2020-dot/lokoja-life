## PART I — THE ONE-PARAGRAPH TRUTH

The reference player is a Nigerian teen on a mid-range Android in afternoon sun — the 360 px
readability law exists because THAT player is the product. Accessibility here is not a settings
page added late; it is the A-1 law (readable before beautiful), the A-3 law (senses agree — every
audio cue has a visual twin and vice versa), the tier walls (kids are structurally safe, not
advisedly safe), and the honest-device economics (heat, data, heap budgets as user-facing truths).

## PART II — THE LAWS ALREADY BINDING (consolidated)

1. **A-1 — Readable before beautiful:** 360 px phone in afternoon sun is the reference device;
   legibility wins every time; the harmattan grade has an explicit readability test
   (Lighting_Bible §V.6: the haze must not eat the HUD).
2. **A-3 — The senses agree:** what the eye says the ear must confirm — rain needs zinc sound AND
   puddles; power loss needs the idiom AND the dark interior. A senses conflict is a bug of meaning.
3. **M13 the family shield (built, battery-proven):** age band at creation (kid/teen/adult); parent
   PIN gate; kid = emotes-only chat, no voice/video/DM-from-strangers, no money (server-side walls,
   11 direct minor-wall sites on the wire); teen = 140 chars + word wall, no money tables; elder
   mode for reduced-intensity play (m13-shots evidence); scammer-round literacy training built.
4. **M9 identity:** handle + PIN + scrypt; no email/phone requirement (contact nullable) — the
   signup wall is passable without owning an email address or sharing a phone number [EXT framing].
5. **Guest honesty:** the 60-second preview wall states what it gates; no dark patterns — the claim
   sheet explains the walls before they bite (M24-W1 register evidence).
6. **Failure states narrate:** every refusal carries a why + next move (EX-40.1); tampered saves
   fail closed WITH an explanation, never a silent wipe.

## PART III — DEVICE & ENVIRONMENT HONESTY [EXT]

1. **Tier truth:** low tier = 30 Hz FramePacer + reduced effects, stated in-device (perfProbe) —
   never pretend the device is something it is not; dynamic-resolution walk is invisible, tier
   labels are not.
2. **Heat & battery:** video calls and stage viewing default OFF on low tier (N-laws + M24 §8.5) —
   the honest choice, labelled in copy.
3. **Data:** wire ≤900 KB budget (SILK) + SW precache; degraded lane keeps the game playable when
   the socket dies — connectivity is an accessibility axis and the game already treats it as one.
4. **Offline:** PWA shell + offline.html; saves are local-first with cloud as the optional lane.

## PART IV — INPUT & CONTROL [EXT]

1. **Dual scheme parity:** touch (joystick + pin) and keyboard (WASD + E + C/V) are first-class;
   any new interaction ships both paths the same day (Interaction_Bible VI).
2. **Freeze = focus:** overlays freezing world input is ALSO an accessibility feature — stray input
   can never act behind a dialog; the same law protects distracted players.
3. **Sprint is optional pacing:** 4.4 u/s walk is sufficient for every wired path; energy decay
   pauses on settle — no interaction requires sprint stamina (walking is never punished).
4. **Motion comfort:** camera modes are offers (A-15); the arrival veil is 2 s and interruptible;
   no forced camera motion exists anywhere — motion-sickness-safe by construction.

## PART V — SENSORY REDUNDANCY [EXT — the pairing law]

Every world-truth event must be perceptible by at least two of sight / sound / text:
| Event | Sight | Sound | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power loss | lights die, interior darkens | S3 idiom ≤3 s | banner/why-map |
| Rain onset | particles + puddles | zinc swell | weather tag |
| Flood day | brown torrent, closure | river layer | canoe banner |
| Market peak | crowd density | dB curve | market-day badge |
| 22:00 trim | lamp pools dominate | gain step | night chip |
| Grid restore | lights re-flow | spool-up idiom | "Up NEPA" moment (S3) |
A future event that cannot fill two columns is redesigned, not shipped.

## PART VI — COGNITIVE & LANGUAGE [EXT]

1. **The greeting clock teaches:** chains are consistent per slot and register — learnable in one
   session (NPC_Bible IV.1); no interaction requires prior Nigerian-language knowledge (English +
   Pidgin carry all systems copy).
2. **Numbers carry anchors:** prices show naira values with dated anchors, never vague scarcity
   metaphors alone — the economy is learnable from its receipts (Economy_Bible VII.5).
3. ** literacy through play:** the scammer-round (M13) trains fraud recognition — accessibility
   includes knowing when the phone is lying to you.
4. **Copy length law:** why-maps stay one breath (EX-40.1); UI strings never exceed two lines at
   360 px.

## PART VII — GATES

1. A-1 explicit test: 360 px × afternoon sun × harmattan grade readability pass per milestone
   (M7 criterion).
2. Tier-wall audit: every social/money surface re-applies kid/teen walls server-side (Content_Expansion
   II.3 reviewer-enforced).
3. Senses-agree flip test per §V row: kill the audio, the sight+text pair still communicates the
   event; kill the visual, sound+text do (A-3 testable).
4. Two-scheme completion: every wired interaction driven end-to-end on touch-only and keyboard-only.
5. Guest path: a newcomer reaches first real interaction without email, phone number, or prior
   knowledge of Nigeria (M1 cold-start criterion extension).
