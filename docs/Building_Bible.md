## PART I — THE ONE-PARAGRAPH TRUTH (carried from 03)

Lokoja is a zinc-roof city on red laterite: 1–2 storey boxes in walled compounds, burglar-barred
windows near-universal, the gate taller than the wall and facing the street, paint and rust writing
age on every surface, and white/green/gold institutional blocks standing apart. Markets grow from
stalls to lock-ups to one-roof halls. Nothing is ornamental that is not functional — a water tank,
a DStv dish, a meter board, a burglar bar are the ornament.

## PART III — MATERIAL & COLOUR LAW (consolidated + v3 extensions)

1. **Zinc tells time (S2):** weathered `#9AA0A3` → painted red-oxide `#B0552F` → rust streaks →
   patched mismatch; blue ridge-cap accent. Zinc is the roof of record; tiles/slabs mark institutions.
2. **Burglar-bar grammar (S3):** instanced vertical-rod vs cross-hatch, gauge = age/wealth signal.
3. **Compound rule (S4):** wall 2–2.5 m, gate taller, gate = the face, ridge glass (NG).
4. **Institutional palette (S9):** white/cream + dark-green trim + gold signage (03 §II.4).
5. **Drainage visible (S6):** open gutters on market spines; the Ultra-Modern enlarged drain point.
6. **[EXT] The door is a panel grammar (v3-compound2, TilesNg):** Nigerian steel security doors carry
   embossed basket-weave/ribbed panel patterns (black/grey powder-coat). Render as instanced
   panel-emboss variants on gates and front doors — the door is the compound's jewellery.
7. **[EXT] Commissioning text is a wall layer (v3-school3, Vanguard):** institutional blocks carry
   hand-painted commissioner labels ("UBEC/SUBEB … EN. WORKS 3RD QRT 2013/086/2017") and pasted
   paper notices that sun-bleach and peel. New facade layer: `commissionText` + `paperPostings`,
   age-graded like murals (v2-sign1/2). Government and school builds gain their dated human record.
8. **[EXT] The dado band generalizes (v2-school1 + v3-school3):** dark-green dado + cream/ochre field
   + blue fascia on public blocks; moss gradient at the wall base = the "paint tells time" law on
   render walls, not just zinc.
9. **[EXT] AC-unit walls and party plazas (v3-civic2, Channels TV — Abuja-verified):** commercial
   blocks sprout external AC compressors on every floor line and rent their facades to signage and
   political banners. NG-labelled dressing for plaza/commercial typology.
10. **[EXT] Palace gate form (v3-compound1, Kano-verified):** monumental crenellated arched gate in
    red/cream — form reference ONLY for the Maigari's palace district dressing; never citable as
    Lokoja residential; the compound-sheet-gate rule (S4) remains the residential law.

## PART IV — INTERIOR LAW [EXT — consolidated from repo + v3 evidence]

1. **The chemist is shelves to the ceiling (v3-pharm2, Alamy):** wooden cubby grid, every cell packed
   with drug boxes, proprietor seated inside the grid. Interior kit: shelf-cells + box-colour noise.
2. **The pharmacy storefront is a fascia board (v3-pharm1, Lagos-verified):** painted fascia with RC
   number + service list, poster paper density on pillars and glass, burglar-gated forecourt.
3. **The market hall is a shed (v3-market1, Pexels):** timber posts + purlins under old zinc, long
   wooden tables, produce in enamel bowls and woven baskets, table-height grammar (waist + floor).
4. **The classroom bar interior (v3-school1 UBE desks + v3-school2 ICIR):** dark dual-desks with
   carved graffiti; the spectrum runs from desk rows (funded) to floor-sitting in peeling mint-green
   rooms (under-resourced) — the AGILE renovation arc's two ends, both evidenced.
5. **The buka is a pot-wall (v3-buka1, Vanguard):** stacked aluminum pots + enamel basins of prepared
   food, vendor in apron over ankara, wooden bench tables kerb-side, tarp/zinc canopy, steam.
6. **Home interiors** remain governed by the repo's interior suites (`interiors.ts` 48 unit suites +
   20 school builders) and Art_Audio A-13 (interior sun law); v3-interior1 (HOLD) supplies textile/
   rattan palette hints for middle-class dressing only — never documentary.
