## PART I — THE ONE-PARAGRAPH TRUTH

A future city is not a content pack; it is a research program. It earns existence the way Lokoja
did: repository audit → independent specialist teams → Red Team → visual verification → evidence
consolidation → bibles → dossier → roadmap → gate. The engine is single-source and shared; the
identity is per-city and non-transferable; "generic African city" content is forbidden the way it
is forbidden here.

## PART II — THE ADMISSION GATE [EXT — before any design work]

A city proposal passes admission only with: (1) a named real place and its research-gate plan
(shaped exactly like this phase), (2) a bible-fork plan listing every book this phase produced
(World, Experience-access, Environment, Lighting, Audio, Building, NPC, Vehicle, Economy, Weather,
Wildlife, UI Philosophy, Interaction, Accessibility, Multiplayer, Content) with its per-city owner,
(3) the identity-anchor candidates named (see §IV), (4) a named source plan (local press, government
pages, peer-reviewed studies, resident consultation). No admission, no art, no design, no code.

## PART III — THE FORK PROCEDURE [EXT]

1. **Fork the books, never the canon:** each per-city bible starts as a skeleton citing the Lokoja
   run as METHOD (not as content); the Lokoja bibles stay untouched and remain the reference
   implementation.
2. **Shared systems stay single-source:** engine, kobo chain, relay doctrine, weather scalars,
   density tables, diegetic laws, N-gates, SILK — one implementation, per-city parameters.
3. **Per-city overrides declare themselves:** sun clock (a new latitude recomputes `solar-output`
   via the same script family), flora palette, fauna census, mode catalogue, wage/price anchors,
   festival calendar, greeting banks — each has a named Lokoja precedent and must be re-derived
   from the new city's evidence.
4. **The adjudication discipline transfers:** A-numbered binding decisions + X-numbered
   contradiction register + honest open-questions ledger from day one (the A7 regatta precedent:
   deviations from reality are conscious, logged, and dressed).

## PART V — THE NAMING & CONTENT LAWS (Content_Expansion III.5–6, expanded)

1. **Engine-generic systems never take a city's name** (kobo-chain, relay, weather scalars stay
   cityless); city-specific named systems (streets, foods, festivals, unions) come only from that
   city's research.
2. **No content migrates by copy-paste:** a Lagos okada is not a Lokoja okada — fares, union names,
   conduct codes, crisis multipliers re-research; shared vehicle CLASSES may share the engine's
   handling law, never its constants.
3. **Populations stay qualitative** until a sourced census lands (the B2 precedent).
4. **Disputes ship as content** (the founding-year and etymology precedents) — a new city's live
   arguments are its cheapest authenticity.

## PART VI — ECONOMY & SCALE LAW [EXT]

1. A second city starts on the shared naira with ITS OWN anchor table (wage floor, staple basket,
   rent direction, POS reality) — dated, Red-Teamed, D-numbered deltas against the repo.
2. Cross-city travel (if ever built) is intercity content: real fares, real parks, real jam
   corridors — researched, never invented; until then cities do not exchange players.
3. The nation wire is multi-city-READY (topics are street-scoped, not city-scoped) but no cross-city
   co-presence ships before each city passes its own gate + the N-gates re-run at doubled CCU budgets.

## PART VII — THE PRECEDENT REGISTER (what the Lokoja run proved transfers)

The nine-team structure (geography / architecture / human-life / transport / culture / economy /
audio / visual-reference / current-reality) + Red Team classification (VERIFIED/SUPPORTED/WEAK/
CONTRADICTED/UNVERIFIABLE) + contamination checks + reproducible geometry scripts + the
visual-verdict discipline (ACCEPT/HOLD/REJECT with manual inspection) + the D1–D-style repo-delta
table + the W/M roadmap shape — all transfer as METHOD. Every Lokoja FACT transfers as nothing.

## PART VIII — GATES

1. No second-city file, art, or constant may enter the repo before its research gate passes
   (Content_Expansion V.3 — reviewer-enforced).
2. The forked bible set must show per-city sources on every load-bearing row before build.
3. The five identity anchors re-derived and the stranger test re-judged locally — by a local.
4. The Lokoja bibles diff clean against their pre-fork state (the fork touches nothing behind it).
