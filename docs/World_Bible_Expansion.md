## PART I — THE CITY IN HISTORY (what a resident would know)

1. **Founded at the confluence.** William Balfour Baikie established the modern town at the Niger–
   Benue confluence — Wikipedia says 1857 (at the site of the 1841 model-farm expedition), Britannica
   says 1860 after the late-1850s trading post (BOTH now live-verified; Part C-1). **Ship the range
   1857–1860; the dispute is dialogue.**
2. **Capital of the British Northern Protectorate (1900–)** — the canonical phrasing (A10). Never
   "first capital of Nigeria" in encyclopaedia copy; NPCs may boast it and be argued with.
3. **Mount Patti is the cradle and the name-shrine.** First primary school in Northern Nigeria (1865)
   on the hill; Lugard ruled from here; **by tradition** Flora Shaw, looking over the confluence from
   Patti, coined "Nigeria" (Times, 1897) — phrase as heritage tradition, never documented on-site
   (Red Team verdict u caveat).
4. **The colonial furniture stands.** Lugard's Rest House on Patti summit: **stone-built, parts
   repaired with modern materials, two Lugard statues flanking** [NEW — S-G]. European cemetery,
   museum, colonial bungalow quarter (Historic Lokoja district) — existence V, forms NG unless noted.
5. **Kogi State's capital since 1991** (state creation); the city is the meeting point of peoples
   from every direction — which is the point of the place.

## PART II — THE PEOPLE & SOCIETY

1. **A confluence of nations.** Indigenous core: Bassa-Nge, Oworo, Nupe. Joined by Hausa, Ebira,
   Igala, Igbo, Edo, Tiv, Okun-Yoruba quarters. No single group owns the city — "Na we meet point".
2. **Belonging = generations, not ethnicity.** "My grandfather sell fish for Kabawa — we be natives
   here, no be settlers." Fifth-to-seventh-generation families are natives regardless of origin.
3. **Language layering (MED):** Hausa = street/market lingua franca; ethnic tongue at home; **Pidgin
   = the playful cross-ethnic bridge**; English formal. Hausa-named old quarters (Kabawa, Makera,
   Ungwan-Pawa) anchor the map.
4. **The name is a live argument:** Oworo "Ilu Oke Oja" · Hausa "Loko Ja" · Nupe "Patti Lukongi" ·
   the loko-tree story (red sap; Lokongoma = "tenth loko"). NPCs argue; the UI never rules.
5. **Respect machinery:** prostrate/kneel greetings to elders; the Maigari is THE central stool with
   sub-stools (Onu Igala Lokoja, Odeyani Anegbira, Olu of Oworo, Ohinoyi for Ebira) — never invent
   titles, never skip a stool's style.
6. **Greeting banks [NEW]:** Hausa timed chains (Ina kwana / Ina wuni / Ina yini / Barka da dare) are
   HIGH; Igala time-of-day greetings verified community-sourced (Part C-8): **Wọ́la òdùdu** /
   **Wọ́lọ ọ̀rọ́ka** / **Wọ́lọ ánẹ́** + **Agba/Awa** (thank you) — MED, usable. Ebira/Nupe/Bassa-Nge
   formulas remain P1 debts; never invented.
7. **Population (qualitative in-game):** 2006 LGA census **196,643** (NPC/NBS); 2022 city-side
   ≈265,000; wider 2020 figure 692,050 [NEW — C-2]. No verified 2026 figure exists; in-game copy
   never prints a current population number.
8. **Median age ~18:** the city is young — crowds skew youth, elders are scarce and weighty.
