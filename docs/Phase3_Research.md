### C-1 · Founding date conflict X2 — RESOLVED AS DOCUMENTED RANGE (both sources now live-verified)
- Britannica (fetched 2026-10-22): *"British merchants established a trading post at the Benue-Niger
  confluence in the late 1850s, and in **1860** the Scottish explorer William Balfour Baikie founded
  Lokoja."* — the previously un-verifiable Britannica leg is now verified verbatim.
- Wikipedia (live extract, re-verified by Red Team earlier today): *"founded in **1857** by William
  Balfour Baikie… at the site of an earlier model farm constructed during the failed Niger expedition
  of 1841."* Wikipedia's category: *"Populated places established in 1857"*.
- **Adjudication (strengthens A4):** the documented range **1857–1860** stands, now with BOTH primary
  encyclopedias live-verified. NPC dialogue may carry the dispute; UI never states one year. No change
  to game doctrine; the conflict is preserved as content.

### C-2 · Population X6 — RESOLVED to the authoritative figure
- `citypopulation.de/en/nigeria/admin/kogi/NGA023012__lokoja/` (fetched 2026-10-22, citing National
  Population Commission + NBS): **Lokoja LGA 2006 census = 196,643; 2022 projection = 265,000**.
- Consequence: team-3's 195,261 AND team-9's 196,988 were both transcription drifts; the city's
  infobox figure **692,050 (as of 2020, Statoids via Wikipedia)** is the wider LGA/metro-side number,
  and *"more than 265,000 (2022)"* is the city-side number.
- **Adjudication (refines A5):** fact sheet uses **2006 LGA census 196,643 (NPC/NBS)** and keeps
  populations qualitative in-game (no verified 2026 city figure exists).
