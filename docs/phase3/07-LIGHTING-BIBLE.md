## PART III — NIGHT LAW

1. **Dark by ≈19:15** year-round (civil twilight ends 18:44–19:14); the 492 lamps + fire-glow + phone
   lights carry the city (visual refs: conf1 shoreline dark, camp lamps in flood rows).
2. **Moonlight:** full-moon tropical night is bright enough to silhouette Patti; moon-phase system
   BLOCKED until the source pass lands (02-S14) — until then, fixed moon = MED debt, recorded.
3. **Harmattan nights:** moon = ringed glow, stars gone (DI from dust optics, labelled); clear dry nights
   show stars (light-pollution quantification = open question).
4. **Interior law:** warm bulb pools through burglar bars (03 §II grammar); generator-dependent shops
   flicker on grid loss (audio S2/S3 own the sound; light follows the same power state).
